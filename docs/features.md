# EduGest — Features and Functional Modules

## 1. Overview

EduGest is organized around different operational areas of school management.

The platform brings academic, administrative and financial activities into a centralized environment, allowing institutional users to manage information through dedicated modules.

This document describes the main functional areas identified in the application.

---

## 2. Dashboard and Operational Overview

The dashboard provides an overview of relevant school information and operational indicators.

**Main capabilities:**
- Access to summarized institutional information.
- Visualization of operational indicators.
- Quick access to application modules.
- Dashboard data visualization.

The dashboard serves as an entry point for monitoring school operations.

---

## 3. Academic Management

The academic management area organizes information related to students, classes, courses, subjects and academic performance.

### 3.1 Student Management

Provides functionality for managing student records.

**Main operations:**
- Student registration.
- Student information management.
- Access to student records.
- Organization of student information.

### 3.2 Class Management

Supports the organization and management of school classes.

**Main operations:**
- Class registration and management.
- Organization of classes.
- Association of students with classes.

### 3.3 Course Management

Organizes the courses offered by the institution.

**Main operations:**
- Course registration.
- Course information management.
- Organization of academic offerings.

### 3.4 Subject Management

Supports the organization of subjects associated with the academic structure.

**Main operations:**
- Subject registration.
- Subject information management.
- Organization of subjects within the academic structure.

### 3.5 Academic Records and Grades

Provides functionality for managing academic performance information.

**Main operations:**
- Grade management.
- Access to academic records.
- Organization of academic information.

---

## 4. Human Resources Management

EduGest includes modules for managing the people involved in school operations.

### 4.1 Teacher Management

Supports the management of teacher information and related institutional records.

### 4.2 Employee Management

Provides functionality for organizing employee records and administrative information.

### 4.3 User Management

Supports the administration of application users and access-related information.

These modules help centralize institutional personnel information.

---

## 5. Financial Management

The financial area supports the management of tuition fees, payments and cash operations.

### 5.1 Tuition Fee Management

Provides functionality for managing student tuition fee information.

### 5.2 Payment Management

Supports the registration and management of payment records.

### 5.3 Cash Management

Organizes cash-related operations and financial records.

These capabilities bring important financial workflows into the same platform used for academic administration.

---

## 6. Communication and Institutional Information

The platform includes communication-related functionality through its announcements and information modules.

### 6.1 Announcements and Notice Board

Provides an area for publishing and managing institutional announcements.

### 6.2 Class Information

Supports the organization of information associated with school classes.

The purpose is to make relevant institutional information accessible through the application.

---

## 7. Administrative and Institutional Configuration

EduGest includes administrative functionality for managing institutional settings and platform operations.

Identified capabilities include:

- School configuration.
- User administration.
- Invitation-based registration workflows.
- Subscription plan management.
- Administrative controls.
- Maintenance and access restriction pages.

These modules support the configuration and administration of the platform.

---

## 8. Data Import, Export and Backup

The application includes utilities for data portability and operational data management.

**Identified capabilities:**
- Spreadsheet-related operations.
- PDF document generation.
- Data export.
- Backup-related functionality.

These tools support administrative reporting and data management workflows.

---

## 9. Access and User Experience

The application includes separate pages and interfaces for different operational areas.

Its structure supports access to academic, financial and administrative functionality through dedicated interfaces.

Authentication and authorization mechanisms are intended to control access to protected operations.

Actual permission enforcement must be evaluated at the backend level rather than inferred from the visibility of frontend pages.

---

## 10. Functional Scope Summary

| Functional Area | Main Modules |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Operational overview |
| Academic Management | Students, classes, courses, subjects, grades |
| Human Resources | Teachers, employees, users |
| Financial Management | Tuition fees, payments, cash |
| Communication | Announcements, class information |
| Administration | School configuration, plans, invitations |
| Data Management | Import, export, backups |

---

## Conclusion

EduGest combines several essential school management operations within a unified web application.

Its functional scope extends beyond basic student registration, covering academic organization, institutional administration, financial workflows and information management.

The platform was developed to address interconnected operational needs through a centralized software solution.
